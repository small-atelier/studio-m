A token kit covering both Skaven Spearheads, *Gnawfeast Clawpack* and *Warpspark Clawpack*: hex tokens for regiment and enhancement picks, damage trays (including a double tray for the Warp Lightning Cannon), a carry box with a Horned Rat lid, and Skaven-badged measuring gauges. The same kit exists for [Helsmiths of Hashut]({{< ref "/printables/hashut-game-tokens" >}}) and [Blades of Khorne]({{< ref "/printables/khorne-tokens" >}}).

The two Skaven Spearheads:

- **Gnawfeast Clawpack:** Clawlord on Gnaw-beast, Grey Seer, Warlock Engineer, 2× 10 Clanrats, 3 Rat Ogors
- **Warpspark Clawpack:** Grey Seer, 3 Stormfiends, Warp Lightning Cannon, 2× 10 Clanrats

Skaven have no token resource of their own, like the Blood Tithe or Daemonic Power. What gets forgotten mid-game is which regiment ability and enhancement were picked, and whether a once-per-battle ability has been spent. The tokens cover exactly that.

## What's in the kit

**Hex tokens** (33mm, pointy-top): the front shows the effect (Horned Rat rune, a big value and a short label). The back shows the ability's name. Once-per-battle abilities have the Horned Rat head on the back instead: flip the token when the ability is spent.

| Spearhead | Token | Kind | Front | Back |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Gnawfeast | Unleashed Warp-Fury | Rat Ogors, once per battle | +1 ATK | Horned Rat |
| Gnawfeast | Warpstone-Laced Bullets | regiment, once per battle | MW CRIT | Horned Rat |
| Gnawfeast | Too Quick to Hit-hit | regiment | NO MW | TOO QUICK |
| Gnawfeast | Lead the Seething Horde | enhancement | 13" REINF | LEAD THE HORDE |
| Gnawfeast | Skryre Connections | enhancement | 2D6 PISTOL | SKRYRE |
| Gnawfeast | Warpstone Charm | enhancement | -1 SAVE | WARP CHARM |
| Gnawfeast | Cloak of Stitched Victories | enhancement | 5+ WARD | CLOAK |
| Warpspark | Warpstone-Laced Armour | regiment, once per battle | 5+ WARD | Horned Rat |
| Warpspark | Endless Swarm of Rats | regiment | D6 RETURN | ENDLESS SWARM |
| Warpspark | Skilled Manipulator | enhancement | 4+ WARD | SKILLED |
| Warpspark | Skitterleap | enhancement | 6+ LEAP | SKITTER LEAP |
| Warpspark | Cage of Warp Lightning | enhancement, once per battle | 2+ CAGE | Horned Rat |
| Warpspark | Scurry Away | enhancement | 3+ FLEE | SCURRY AWAY |

Before the game, pull the regiment token and the enhancement token for the chosen picks and put them beside the general. The Warp-Fury token goes beside the Rat Ogors.

**Damage trays:** an arrow-shaped plate, 22×40×6.5mm, with a pocket for one 16mm d6. The die shows the damage on the unit's currently wounded model, and the arrow points at the unit. The Warp Lightning Cannon has 8 Health and can carry 7 damage, so it gets a **double tray** (41×40mm) with two die pockets side by side.

| Tray | Used by | Print |
|---|---|---|
| Single | Clawlord, Grey Seer, Warlock Engineer, Rat Ogors / Grey Seer, Stormfiends | ×4 |
| Double | Warp Lightning Cannon | ×1 |

A single tray also works for *Will of the Horned Rat*: set the die to the roll and park the tray beside the target unit until the start of your next turn.

## The box

The box has the same footprint as the Hashut and Khorne boxes (165.5×131.2×24.5mm) and uses the same column layout. Every well is 22mm deep:

- **Left column:** 3 stacked hex wells, 7 tokens each: Gnawfeast picks, Warpspark picks, spares. Each well has a lift notch through the left wall.
- **Right column, front:** a 123×73mm slot for the warscroll cards, with a notch through the front wall. The floor carries a cards + die icon.
- **Right column, back:** 2 arrow-shaped wells holding 3 single trays each, a double-tray well, and a closed dice well (24×51mm, 3 spare d6 in a row). The tray wells are notched through the back wall.

**Lid:** it slips over the base with a full-height skirt. The top carries the Horned Rat head with the rune in each corner. The inside face carries a dice icon: flip the lid over and it's a dice tray.
