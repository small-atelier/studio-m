The Helforge Host: War Despot, Dominator Engine (Immolation Cannons), Tormentor Bombard, 2× 5 Infernal Cohort with Hashutite Spears (one with the Gong Carrier).

The Spearhead runs on a simpler Daemonic Power rule than the Battletome: at the start of each of your hero phases, *Harness Daemonic Power* clears every unit's points and gives you the battle round number plus 1 to allocate, at most 3 per unit. There are no Desolation Tokens. The War Despot's *Black-Hearted Conqueror* makes every unit near him count as having 1 point anyway, so points end up stacked on a few units.

What gets forgotten mid-game: each unit's current power level, which regiment ability and enhancement were picked, whether a once-per-battle ability has been spent, and which enemy unit is still slowed by *Suppressive Bombardment*. The daemon engines also carry a lot of damage.

## What's in the kit

**Daemonic Power tokens** (33mm hex): the DPP 1/2/3 tokens from the [full Age of Sigmar kit]({{< ref "/printables/aos/hashut-game-tokens" >}}). One token per level, swapped when a unit's level changes. 2 of each covers a Spearhead game: 5 points at most per turn, and the Despot's aura makes spreading single points pointless.

**Hex tokens** (33mm, pointy-top): the front shows the effect (Hashut rune, a big value and a short label). The back shows the ability's name. Once-per-battle abilities have the bull skull on the back instead: flip the token when the ability is spent.

| Token | Kind | Front | Back |
|---|---|---|---|
| Grinding Advance | regiment, once per battle | 3" MOVE | bull |
| Suppressive Bombardment | regiment, lingering | -1 CHARGE | SUPPRESS BOMBARD |
| Scroll of Petrification | enhancement, once per battle | 2+ WARD | bull |
| Chalice of Darkness | enhancement, once per battle | D3 MW | bull |
| Talisman of Obsidian | enhancement | 1st IGNORE | TALISMAN |
| Amulet of Burning Hate | enhancement, once per battle | 5+ CRIT | bull |
| Strike the Gongs | Gong Carrier unit, once per battle | D3 RETURN | bull |

Before the game, pull the regiment token and the enhancement token for the chosen picks and put them beside the general. The Strike the Gongs token goes beside the Cohort with the Gong Carrier.

**Suppressive Bombardment** goes beside the enemy target: until the start of your next turn, it rolls one fewer dice for charges (minimum 1).

**Damage trays:** the arrow-shaped plate from the other kits, with a pocket for one 16mm d6 per tray. The die shows the damage on the unit's currently wounded model, and the arrow points at the unit. The engines need a **double tray** (two die pockets side by side): the Dominator Engine has 10 Health and can carry 9 damage, the Tormentor Bombard 8 and 7.

| Tray | Used by | Print |
|---|---|---|
| Single | War Despot | ×1 |
| Double | Dominator Engine, Tormentor Bombard | ×2 |

Infernal Cohort have 1 Health and don't need one.

## The box

The box has the same footprint as the other Spearhead boxes (165.5×131.2×24.5mm) and uses the same column layout. Every well is 22mm deep:

- **Left column:** 3 stacked hex wells, one per DPP level. Each well has a lift notch through the left wall.
- **Right column, front:** a 123×73mm slot for the warscroll cards, with a notch through the front wall. The floor carries a cards + die icon.
- **Right column, back:** a double-tray well holding all three trays (a single tray nests inside the double's outline), a hex well for the reminder tokens, and a closed dice well.

The underside carries the Mythos logo, inlaid flush.

**Lid:** it slips over the base with a full-height skirt. The top carries the bull skull with the Hashut rune in each corner. The inside face carries a dice icon: flip the lid over and it's a dice tray.
