The Helforge Host: War Despot, Dominator Engine (Immolation Cannons), Tormentor Bombard, 2× 5 Infernal Cohort with Hashutite Spears (one with the Gong Carrier).

The Spearhead runs on a simpler Daemonic Power rule than the Battletome: at the start of each of your hero phases, *Harness Daemonic Power* clears every unit's points and gives you the battle round number plus 1 to allocate, at most 3 per unit. There are no Desolation Tokens. The War Despot's *Black-Hearted Conqueror* makes every unit near him count as having 1 point anyway, so points end up stacked on a few units.

What gets forgotten mid-game: each unit's current power level, which regiment ability and enhancement were picked, whether a once-per-battle ability has been spent, and which enemy units are still slowed or stripped of control. The daemon engines also carry a lot of damage.

## What's in the kit

**Daemonic Power tokens** (33mm hex): the DPP 1/2/3 tokens from the [full Age of Sigmar kit]({{< ref "/printables/aos/hashut-game-tokens" >}}). One token per level, swapped when a unit's level changes. 2 of each covers a Spearhead game: 5 points at most per turn, and the Despot's aura makes spreading single points pointless.

**Ability tokens** (56×30mm, the plate of the Desolation token): one per regiment ability and enhancement, plus the Gong Carrier's *Strike the Gongs*. Text only, in a condensed font that keeps letters open at small size.

- **Front:** the ability's name on the first two rows, a short version of the rule on the third. It prints on top.
- **Back:** who it applies to (ARMY, GENERAL or COHORT), then when it's used.

Before the game, pull the regiment and enhancement tokens for the chosen picks and keep them with the warscroll cards. The Strike the Gongs token goes beside the Cohort with the Gong Carrier. Flip a once-per-battle token when it's used.

Regiment abilities, back reads ARMY:

| Ability | Rule (front) | When (back) |
|---|---|---|
| Grinding Advance | NO CHARGE ROUND 1 | ONCE PER BATTLE · DEPLOYMENT |
| Suppressive Bombardment | -1 CHARGE DIE | ONCE PER TURN · SHOOTING PHASE |

*Grinding Advance* moves up to 2 Cohorts 3" during deployment; the token is there for the part that lasts: those units can't charge in the first battle round. *Suppressive Bombardment* goes beside the enemy target: until the start of your next turn, it rolls one fewer dice for charges (minimum 1).

Enhancements, back reads GENERAL:

| Enhancement | Rule (front) | When (back) |
|---|---|---|
| Scroll of Petrification | WARD 2+ NO ABILITY | ONCE PER BATTLE · ANY HERO PHASE |
| Chalice of Darkness | D3 MW WITHIN 12" | ONCE PER BATTLE · SHOOTING PHASE |
| Talisman of Obsidian | IGNORE 1ST DMG/PHASE | PASSIVE |
| Amulet of Burning Hate | CRIT ON 5+ THIS TURN | ONCE PER BATTLE · COMBAT PHASE |

The Scroll lasts the rest of the turn: the general can't use abilities or be picked by friendly abilities while it's active.

Infernal Cohort warscroll, back reads COHORT:

| Ability | Rule (front) | When (back) |
|---|---|---|
| Strike the Gongs | REMOVE GONG: D3 BACK | ONCE PER BATTLE · HERO PHASE |

**Control tokens** (33mm hex, ×4): the Hashut rune, *-3* and *CONTROL*, the same on both faces. *Ruinous Bombardment* on the Tormentor Bombard takes 3 off the control score of the unit it shot and up to one more unit within 6" per daemonic power point on the Bombard, on a 3+ each. Put a token beside every unit it lands on and clear them at the end of the turn.

**Damage trays:** an arrow-shaped plate, 22×40×6.5mm, with a pocket for one 16mm d6. The die shows the damage on the unit's currently wounded model, and the arrow points at the unit. The engines get a **double tray** (41×40mm) with two die pockets side by side: the Dominator Engine has 10 Health and can carry 9 damage, the Tormentor Bombard 8 and 7.

| Tray | Used by | Print |
|---|---|---|
| Single | War Despot | ×1 |
| Double | Dominator Engine, Tormentor Bombard | ×2 |

Infernal Cohort have 1 Health and don't need one.

## The box

The box has the same footprint as the other Spearhead boxes (165.5×131.2×24.5mm) and uses the same column layout. Every well is 22mm deep:

- **Left column:** 3 stacked hex wells for the 6 Daemonic Power and 4 control tokens, 7 tokens each at most. Each well has a lift notch through the left wall.
- **Right column, front:** a 123×73mm slot for the warscroll cards, with a notch through the front wall. The floor carries a cards + die icon.
- **Right column, back:** a well for the 7 ability tokens and an arrow-shaped well for the trays: both doubles, with the single tray nested on top inside the double's outline. Both are notched through the back wall.

The underside carries the Mythos logo, inlaid flush.

{{< figure src="gallery/box_base_underside.png" caption="Underside: Mythos logo" >}}

**Lid:** it slips over the base with a full-height skirt. The top carries the bull skull with the Hashut rune in each corner. The inside face carries a dice icon: flip the lid over and it's a dice tray.
