Board sizes, terrain rules and objectives for Age of Sigmar 4 matched play and Spearhead: the numbers that matter when building boards, terrain and objective markers.
{{< /lead >}}

## Age of Sigmar matched play

From the General's Handbook 2026-27 [G] and the core rules [R].

### Battlefield

| Game                              | Size      | Terrain features |
|-----------------------------------|-----------|-----------------:|
| 1000 or 2000 pts                  | 44" × 60" | 8                |
| 1000 pts, smaller alternative     | 30" × 44" | 4                |
| Other points limits               | scale size and terrain count proportionally | |

### Terrain

- **Mix:** 4 small + 4 medium features.
- **Size bands:** small fits in 7" × 7"; medium fits in 7" × 12"; large is anything bigger. A feature made of several pieces counts as one, and all its pieces must fit its size area.
- **Setup spacing:** more than 3" from the battlefield edge, 3" from every objective, 6" from every other terrain feature. Or placed per the battleplan's terrain map, with the same distances.
- **Types:**

| Type          | Examples                         | Abilities                         |
|---------------|----------------------------------|-----------------------------------|
| Obstacle      | ruins, debris, statues, barricades | Cover, Unstable                 |
| Obscuring     | wyldwood, fortress wall          | Cover, Obscuring, Unstable        |
| Area          | hills, Stormvault                | Cover                             |
| Place of Power | realmgate                       | Cover, Place of Power, Unstable   |

- **Cover:** −1 to hit a unit behind or wholly on the feature, unless it charged this turn or has Fly.
- **Obscuring:** a unit behind or wholly on it can't be shot, unless it has Fly.
- **Unstable:** models can cross it but can't set up or end a move on any part more than 1" tall.
- **Height:** anything up to 1" tall is moved over as if it isn't there; taller, the vertical distance counts toward the move.

For building: tops at 1" or lower stay playable; the 7" × 7" and 7" × 12" footprints are the hard limits for small and medium pieces.

### Objectives

- **Marker:** round, 40mm across. Models can move over and stand on it; it doesn't block visibility.
- **Control zone:** extends 3" from the 40mm centre, so the full zone is a 192mm disc. A unit within the zone contests it.
- **GHB 2026-27 objectives:** six, of four kinds: 2 Sun Seekers, 2 Golden Lions, 1 Heidenhain, 1 Scions of the Comet.
- **Placement:** the defender places them exactly where the battleplan map shows, centred on the icon.

## Spearhead

From the Spearhead section of the core rules [R] and the Warhammer Community announcement [W].

### Battlefield

- **Size:** 30" × 22.4", a quarter of a full 44" × 60" battlefield [W]. The rules text rounds it to 30" × 22" [R].
- **Double-sided:** each realm battlefield has a different realm on each face. Fire and Jade: Aqshy / Ghyran.

### Terrain

- **Count:** 2 large + 2 small features.
- **Setup:** each player sets up 1 large + 1 small wholly in their own territory: more than 6" from other terrain, more than 3" from both long edges and from enemy territory, and never on an objective.
- **Abilities:** large = Cover, Obscuring, Unstable; small = Cover, Unstable.
- **Pieces named in the rules:** Ruined Domicile (large), Ravaged Shrine (small).

### Objectives

- **Printed on the board:** the objective is the whole printed circle, not just the symbol in its centre.
- **Contesting:** a model standing on it, wholly or partly.
- **Fire and Jade names:** Behemat, Ignax, Dracothion, Vulcatrix, Nagendra.
