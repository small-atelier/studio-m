## Battlefield

| Game                          | Size      | Terrain features |
|-------------------------------|-----------|-----------------:|
| 1000 or 2000 pts              | 44" × 60" | 8                |
| 1000 pts, smaller alternative | 30" × 44" | 4                |

## Terrain

- **Set:** 4 small + 4 medium features.
- **Footprints:** small fits in 7" × 7", medium in 7" × 12". A feature built from several pieces counts as one, and all of them must fit that area.
- **Height:** models can't stand on any part taller than 1", so only tops at 1" or lower are playable.

## Objectives

- **Set:** six objectives per battleplan in the General's Handbook 2026-27.
- **Marker:** a flat 40mm disc with a 3" control zone around it, so the full zone is a 192mm disc. Printable: [AoS objective markers]({{< ref "/printables/aos-objective-markers" >}}).
