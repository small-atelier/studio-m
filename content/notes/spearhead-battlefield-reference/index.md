{{< lead >}}
Board, terrain and objectives to build or buy for Age of Sigmar Spearhead, plus the printed objective rings on the four official realm battlefields.
{{< /lead >}}

## Battlefield

- **Size:** 30" × 22.4", a quarter of a full 44" × 60" battlefield [W]. The rules text rounds it to 30" × 22" [R].
- **Double-sided:** each realm battlefield has a different realm on each face. Fire and Jade: Aqshy / Ghyran.

## Terrain

- **Set:** 2 large + 2 small features.
- **Pieces named in the rules:** Ruined Domicile (large), Ravaged Shrine (small).

## Objectives

Printed on the board as rings, and the whole ring is the objective, so a replacement disc matches the ring size. Printable: [objective marker tiles]({{< ref "/printables/objective-marker-tiles#spearhead-boards" >}}).

## Official realm battlefields

Photographed long edge horizontal. Positions are to the ring centre, from the nearest edges. Each ring takes one 40mm coin; the STLs are on the [Objective Marker Tiles]({{< ref "/printables/objective-marker-tiles#downloads" >}}) page.
