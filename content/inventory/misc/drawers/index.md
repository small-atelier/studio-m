{{< lead >}}
A modular drawer/bin storage system for the desk, built on a found parametric design and resized to fit — including an organizer insert for the sticks/wire used to mount models for painting.
{{< /lead >}}

Started from an existing modular drawer/module STL family and stretched every part by the same amount along its length axis, so the whole set stays interchangeable at the new size. One half-width module got a further pass: floor removed, dividers added, and a label lip — turning it into an open 4-compartment tube sized to hold mounting sticks/wire upright.

Connectors come in three lengths to span different rack widths.
