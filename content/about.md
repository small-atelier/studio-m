# About Studio M

**Studio M // Miniatures & Models** is a personal workshop and documentation space for all things tabletop miniatures, model building, and painting. Here I track my projects, experiment with paint schemes, and record techniques, progress, and ideas.

This site serves both as a **hobby log** for myself and a source of inspiration for other miniature enthusiasts. Every project is documented with:

- Goals and concepts
- Paint schemes and recipes
- Step-by-step progress
- Photos of work in progress and finished models

I also keep track of **inventory, techniques, and notes**, making Studio M a complete miniature workshop notebook in digital form.

Whether you're here to browse completed projects, follow painting techniques, or just enjoy the miniatures, welcome to the studio! 🎨
