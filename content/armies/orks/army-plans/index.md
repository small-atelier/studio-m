## Suggested Army Builds

### Speedwaaagh! — primary pick

Low model count, high speed, plays fast. Roughly:

- [Warboss](https://wahapedia.ru/wh40k11ed/factions/orks/Warboss) (on Warbike once bought, proxy for now)
- [Wazdakka Gutsmek](https://wahapedia.ru/wh40k11ed/factions/orks/Wazdakka-Gutsmek) *(needs buying)*
- [Big Mek](https://wahapedia.ru/wh40k11ed/factions/orks/Big-Mek) (from Armageddon box, once built/painted)
- [Warbikers](https://wahapedia.ru/wh40k11ed/factions/orks/Warbikers) × 8 — Battleline via Wazdakka
- [Wartrakks](https://wahapedia.ru/wh40k11ed/factions/orks/Wartrakk) × 2
- [Buggies](https://wahapedia.ru/wh40k11ed/factions/orks/Warbuggies) × 3 — Warbuggies, **Legends** datasheet; check they're allowed in whatever format/event you're playing before relying on them
- [Deffkoptas](https://wahapedia.ru/wh40k11ed/factions/orks/Deffkoptas) × 3
- [Kommandos](https://wahapedia.ru/wh40k11ed/factions/orks/Kommandos) × 5
- [Meganobz](https://wahapedia.ru/wh40k11ed/factions/orks/Meganobz) × 4 in a Trukk *(needs building/painting)*
- [Mek Gunz](https://wahapedia.ru/wh40k11ed/factions/orks/Mek-Gunz) × 5 (Kustom Mega-Kannon ×3, Smasha Gun, Traktor Kannon)
- [Boyz](https://wahapedia.ru/wh40k11ed/factions/orks/Boyz) × 20 (Shoota, with Big Shoota) — single mob for objectives, leave the other two mobs at home

*(See the [NewRecruit list](https://www.newrecruit.eu/app/list/yBN4E) for exact points/legality.)*
