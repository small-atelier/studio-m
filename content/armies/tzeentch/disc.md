## Paint Scheme

Same recipe as the [Screamers](screamers-paint), plus a white drybrush underpaint for extra shading. See that page for the full write-up.

### Short version

1. **NATO Green** primer
2. **Matt White** drybrush — underpaint, builds up some shading before colour goes on
3. **Crystal Blue** airbrush from below
4. **Alien Purple** airbrush from above
5. **Daemonic Yellow** glow on the nose/front
6. Ink glazes — **blue** on the underside, **magenta** on the back
