## Approximate Paint Scheme (reverse-engineered)

Recipe is a guess based on the finished models — not confirmed.

### 1. Prime

**Black primer**

### 2. Drybrush — Round 1

**Matt White** — medium-heavy pass over all raised surfaces.

Builds the base for the ink to key into.

### 3. Ink — Magenta (airbrush)

**Liquitex Quinacridone Magenta Ink**

Applied selectively with airbrush — not a full flood. Covers most of the body but let's black stay in the deepest recesses.

### 4. Drybrush — Round 2

**Matt White** again, lighter pass.

Re-brightens the highlights on top of the magenta layer. Creates the mottled, organic skin texture.

### 5. Details — White

This will cover most of the magenta for say the beak, tounge, eyes etc ...

### 5. Ink — Blue (airbrush)

**Liquitex Phthalocyanine Blue Ink**

Light selective pass — shifts the upper highlights slightly cooler and adds depth without going cold overall.

### 6. Drybrush — Round 3

**Matt White**, very light final pass on the highest points only.

### 7. Details — Black

**Black** to pick out recesses between body parts, clean up separations, sharpen the model. Acts as a under coat for gold

### 8. Details — Gold

**Gold** (metallic) for the staves and any jewelry/icons.

### 9. Claws and Teeth

**Skeleton Bone** or light grey — quick pick-out on tips.
