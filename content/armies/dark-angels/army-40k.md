## Notes / Ideas

- Theme: Deathwing/Ravenwing — Terminator-armoured elites backed by a fast bike screen, classic Inner Circle combo.
- Detachment guess: **Inner Circle Task Force** — needs checking against the current Faction Pack once this actually gets built.
- Warlord progression: Interrogator-Chaplain through 500p–2000p, replaced by **Azrael** at 3000p.
- Belial is the Deathwing Terminators' dedicated Leader — attach once there's a squad worth it.
- Land Raider Crusader is the Deathwing's transport once the collection reaches 2000p; Ravenwing stays fast and unembarked throughout.
