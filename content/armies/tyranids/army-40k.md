## Notes / Ideas

- [Neurogaunts](neurogaunts) are cheap synapse-range extension for the Neurotyrant — solid include at almost any points level.
- [Termagants](termagants) → wishlist Hormagaunts is the troop/swarm core; a Tervigon (wishlist) would keep Termagants topped up if the collection grows that way.
- [Broodlord](broodlord) currently has no Genestealer unit sized to actually attach to in a clean list — worth planning which Genestealers split (5s or 10s) pairs with it.
- [Neurotyrant](neurotyrant) or wishlist Hive Tyrant as the single big synapse/psychic piece — likely not both at this collection's size, same logic Tzeentch used for its big single-caster choice.
- [Von Ryan's Leapers](von-ryans-leapers) are the fast ambush element; [Lictor](lictor) plays a similar infiltrate/objective role solo.
- [Screamer-Killer](screamer-killer) is still its own named, separately-priced loadout (125p/135p) on the Carnifexes datasheet — pricier than the plain Carnifex loadout (90p/180p) it shares a datasheet with.
- [Winged Tyranid Prime](winged-tyranid-prime) is a cheap (65p) Leader for [Tyranid Warriors](tyranid-warriors) — worth attaching once a Warriors squad size is settled.
