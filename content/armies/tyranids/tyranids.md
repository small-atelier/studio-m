### Notes & Tips

* **Neurogaunts** are free and great for synapse — always include them to avoid morale.
* **Hormagaunts** are crucial melee units — getting at least one squad for melee pressure is recommended.
* **Hive Tyrant** is your premier HQ; if you want to be competitive, aim to add one soon.
* **Screamer-Killer** and **Tyrannofex** add solid monster support.
* Use **Raveners** and **Von Ryan’s Leapers** for fast flank and disruption.
* Broodlord and Deathleaper boost Genestealers and Lictors, enhancing assassination and psychic control.
