## Army Ideas by Points Level

Two copies of the same box are owned — the Spearhead: Helforge Host and the Helsmiths of Hashut Army Set are the identical 14 models (see [Roster](roster/)) — so the case already holds a full ~940p army with nothing new bought. Brackets below reflect that: 750 and 1,000 come entirely from what's already owned; 1,250 and 2,000 are the first points that need new kits.

Both Infernal Cohort units are built as **Hashutite Spears**, kept as two separate 10-model units — not one reinforced 20, not one Spears/one Blades. Reason: "Conquered Lands" (feeds a DPP to a nearby friendly unit on a 3+ while contesting an objective) is a Spears-only ability that fires once per unit per turn — two separate Spears units sitting on two objectives can trigger it twice a turn, which neither a single 20-block nor a mixed Spears/Blades pair can match. Trade-off: no Anti-Infantry Blades option at all.
