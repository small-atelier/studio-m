## Notes / Ideas

- The owned + assembled pool (Ahriman, Infernal Master, 5 Scarab Occult Terminators, 2 Chaos Spawn, 20 Tzaangors, 40 Rubric Marines) tops out around **1,355p** (cheapest per-model split) — roughly the 1,250p bracket. 1,500p and up need new purchases.
- Detachment: **Rubricae Phalanx**. Rule "All is Dust": +1 to save rolls for `RUBRICAE` units (Rubric Marines and Scarab Occult Terminators) against attacks with an unmodified Damage characteristic of 1. Covers both core owned units directly, always-on (no psychic-test dependency) — picked over the earlier Ritual of Regeneration suggestion, which only touched the Rubric Marines' healing.
- Ahriman and Infernal Master both lead Rubric Marines — pick one as Warlord per bracket, the other rides with a second squad once there are enough bodies to field two.
- Scarab Occult Terminators are the hard-hitting elite unit — Sorcerer in Terminator Armour (Wishlist) is their dedicated Leader once there's room.
- Tzaangors are the cheap screening/objective body — Tzaangor Shaman (Wishlist) leads them and the Enlightened variants.
- 1,500p+ pulls from the [Wishlist](roster#wishlist) — suggestions below are one plausible shopping list, not the only one.
