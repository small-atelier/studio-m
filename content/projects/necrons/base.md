This guide documents the basing process used for our Necron models.
The goal is to create a dry desert-style base with good texture and contrast against the metallic models.

---

## Materials Used

* GW Technical texture paint (Astro Granit)
* Black primer (spray)
* Monster Brown
* Desert Yellow
* Skeleton Bone
* Abaddon Black (base rim)

---

## Step-by-Step Process

### 1. Apply Texture

* Apply **GW Technical texture paint** to the base.
* **Smear it on liberally** to create natural ground texture.
* Use an old brush or sculpting tool to spread it.

### 2. Let It Dry

* Allow the texture paint to **dry completely** before priming.
* This prevents cracking or paint separation.

### 3. Prime

* Spray the entire model and base with **black primer**.
* This creates strong shadows in the texture.

### 4. Base Color

* Paint the base with **Monster Brown**.

### 5. First Dry Brush

* Lightly dry brush **Desert Yellow** over the raised texture.

### 6. Final Highlight

* Apply a lighter dry brush of **Skeleton Bone** to the highest points.

### 7. Base Rim

* Paint the base rim with **Abaddon Black** for a clean finish.

---

## Tips

* Apply texture **before priming** so the primer seals everything together.
* Use **progressively lighter dry brushing** to bring out the ground texture.
* Keep the rim clean — it frames the model and improves presentation.
