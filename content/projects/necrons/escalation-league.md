Mythos 40K escalation league, September 2026 – January 2027. Casual/beginner-friendly, one round per calendar month, points climbing each round. Necrons, primary detachment **Cursed Legion** (Destroyer Cult-heavy lists) — **Hand of the Dynasty** as the alternative for Warriors/Immortals-heavy lists with no Destroyer Cult presence. Full list breakdowns and build notes live on the [40k Army List](army-40k) page — this is just the quick reference.
