## Notes / Ideas

- The owned + assembled pool tops out around **1,540p** (cheapest per-model split) — almost exactly the December 1,500p bracket. Everything through 1,500p below is built from models you already have; nothing to buy until January.
- Detachment: **Cursed Legion** (confirmed via the June 2026 Faction Pack). Rule "Cold Fervour": +2 Strength to all `DESTROYER CULT` weapons (Skorpekh + Ophydian Destroyers); the first time each turn a Destroyer Cult unit destroys a unit or drops it below half strength, every other Necrons unit also gets +2 S until end of turn — this is the payoff for the Necron Warriors blocks. Reaches the whole list, not just the Destroyer half of it, so it's the default across brackets unless a bracket skews hard toward Warriors/Immortals instead (in which case Hand of the Dynasty — Assault on Warriors/Immortals guns — is the alternative).
- Two Overlords means one runs as Warlord glued to the Necron Warriors/Immortals blob, the second either leads a second combat squad (bigger brackets) or stays on the shelf (smaller ones).
- Royal Warden and Plasmancer are the cheap character glue — attach to whichever Warriors/Immortals unit needs the buff.
- Canoptek Reanimator + Scarab Swarms are the durability backbone — keep them near Destroyers/vehicles to heal each turn.
- Night Scythe is the only transport/flyer in the pool — most useful once there's a squad worth deep-striking (Lychguard or Immortals), so it shows up mid-bracket rather than early.
- 2,000p and 3,000p pull from the [Wishlist](roster#wishlist) — suggestions below are just one plausible shopping list, not the only one.
