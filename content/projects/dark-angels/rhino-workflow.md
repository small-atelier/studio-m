### 🎨 Result
> Deep, classic Dark Angels green with rich shadows and natural highlights — fast, consistent, and battle-ready.

---

## 🛡️ Notes for Consistency
- Keep a test card with your base and highlight mix for future color matching.
- Maintain same light direction (45° front-top) across vehicles.
- Thin paints well — smooth layers beat heavy coats every time.

---

## 1️⃣ Prime – Vallejo Negro Primer (Black)
- Apply an even coat with airbrush.
  *(Creates deep shadows and solid base for the armor.)*

---

## 2️⃣ Underpainting – Drybrush White / Light Grey
- Drybrush **from above (~45°)** using **matte white acrylic**, catching raised panels and top edges.
- Optional: add **Vallejo Verde Nato Primer** as a gentle mid-tone transition.  
  *(Gives natural highlights that will show through the green layer.)*

---

\newpage

---

## 3️⃣ Base Color – Dark Angels Green
**Goal:** Deep, rich green with natural shadow and light variation.

**Airbrush Mix:**
- 3 parts **Army Painter Angel Green** (or Army Green for duller tone)
- 1 part **Liquitex Transparent Burnt Umber ink** (adds depth)
- Thin 1:1 with airbrush thinner

**Apply:**
- Spray light, semi-transparent coats.
- Let the underpainting show slightly through the upper areas.
- Build color slowly to preserve contrast.

---

## 4️⃣ Highlight – Zenithal Green Layer
**Mix:**
- 2 parts **Angel Green**
- 1 part **Greenskin or Goblin Green**
- + a few drops **Liquitex Yellow Medium Azo ink** (optional warmth)

**Spray Direction:**
- From above (45° angle), focusing on top surfaces and upper sides.  
  *(Creates natural lighting gradient.)*

---

## 5️⃣ Optional Edge Pop
- Mix **Goblin Green + White (1:1)**
- Light airbrush mist or controlled drybrush on edges and upper panels.  
  *(Sharpens contrast and gives “Dark Angels edge” definition.)*

---

## 6️⃣ Details & Finishing
**Metal:**
- Gun Metal → wash with thinned **Liquitex Carbon Black ink** → drybrush silver.

**Tracks:**
- Gunmetal → black/brown wash → silver edge drybrush.

**Icons & Trim:**
- Bone: Skeleton Bone + White highlight.
- Red: Pure Red → wash (Carbon Black + Magenta) → Lava Orange highlight.

**Lenses:**
- Red (Mephiston-style) or Blue (Liquitex Phthalo Blue ink).

---

## 7️⃣ Seal & Protect
- **Satin varnish** (Army Painter / Vallejo).
- Optional **matte varnish** on armor plates.
- Optional dusting: mix **Verde Nato + Burnt Umber ink**, spray lightly on lower hull for subtle weathering.
