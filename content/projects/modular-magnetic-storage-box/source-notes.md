## Modular Magnetic Miniature Storage Box

A stackable, modular storage and transport system for tabletop miniatures, designed to combine 3D-printed structural components with inexpensive sheet materials such as plywood, acrylic, or thin steel.

Each storage unit consists of a shallow removable tray with a thin ferromagnetic steel plate forming the floor. Miniatures equipped with small magnets in their bases attach directly to the steel surface, keeping them securely positioned during transport while allowing them to be easily removed when needed.

The system is built from small 3D-printable corner pieces, rails, connectors, and stacking interfaces rather than requiring the entire box to be printed. Large flat panels can be cut from plywood or other inexpensive sheet material. This keeps printing time and material usage low while allowing the overall dimensions of the case to be customized.

Each tray has an integrated stacking system. Locating pins, sockets, or interlocking edges prevent trays from sliding sideways when stacked. Multiple trays can therefore be combined into a tall storage tower, while individual trays remain removable and usable as miniature transport trays.

Different tray heights can be produced using the same basic components. A low-profile tray can be used for infantry and small miniatures, while taller versions can accommodate cavalry, monsters, terrain, vehicles, or miniatures with raised scenic bases.

The system should be designed around a standardized footprint and standardized connection points. This allows future components to be added without redesigning the entire case.

Possible components include:

* Standard magnetic miniature tray
* Low-profile infantry tray
* Tall miniature/monster tray
* Vehicle tray
* Removable steel floor
* Plywood or acrylic side panels
* 3D-printed corner brackets
* 3D-printed stacking connectors
* Carrying handle
* Removable or hinged lid
* Magnetic or mechanical latches
* Optional dividers
* Optional foam or padded protective inserts
* Optional label holders for identifying the contents of each tray

The overall design goal is a system that is **modular, inexpensive, printable on a normal desktop 3D printer, easy to repair, easy to expand, and strong enough for transporting painted miniatures**.

The ideal result is a stack of individual miniature trays that can be carried as one case, separated into smaller units for transport, or placed directly on the gaming table when needed.
