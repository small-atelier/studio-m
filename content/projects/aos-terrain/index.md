FDM-printed Age of Sigmar terrain, used on the Spearhead board and on full-size tables.
{{< /lead >}}

## Pieces

[AOS 4 Skaventide Terrain Equivalent](https://www.thingiverse.com/thing:6724571) by ChickenFPV, CC BY-SA: `small_AOS_terrain.stl` + `big_AOS_terrain.stl`. Printed at 0.4mm nozzle, 0.2mm layer height.

Two lava volcanoes from the [Mythos lava set]({{< ref "/projects/mythos-lava-terrain" >}}) at 100%: `21_01_Volcano_01_fixed.stl` and `21_01_Volcano_04_fixed.stl`. Large features, used as the Spearhead large terrain on Aqshy.

## Finishing

Sealed and textured with the acrylic floor-primer mixes from the [floor primer note]({{< ref "/notes/floor-primer-as-hobby-medium" >}}).
