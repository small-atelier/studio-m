Two boxes owned: the **Spearhead: Helforge Host** and the **Helsmiths of Hashut Army Set**. Same 14 miniatures both times — the Army Set just bundles in the Battletome book and 32 warscroll cards, no extra models. So the case holds **two full copies** of everything: 2× War Despot, 2× Dominator Engine sprue, 2× Tormentor Bombard/Deathshrieker sprue, 22× Infernal Cohort. Base sizes verified against the **Portable Hellforge** build notes (`content/posts/portable-hellforge`, still draft).

## Build plan

War Despot and the war machines go one-of-each-variant. The two Infernal Cohort units both go **Hashutite Spears**, kept as two separate 10-model units rather than merged into one 20 or split Spears/Blades — chosen for the DPP angle: "Conquered Lands" (grants a nearby friendly unit a daemonic power point on a 3+ while contesting an objective) is Spears-only and triggers once per unit per turn, so two separate Spears units on two objectives can fire it twice a turn; a single 20-block or a mixed Spears/Blades pair can only ever fire it once. Full reasoning in the [AoS Army List](army-aos/).

**Total across both boxes: 28 models.** No Hashutite Blades built — traded away for the doubled DPP trigger. (Base sizes per the September 2025 Battle Profiles: Dominator Engine is listed there as 80mm, not the 100mm shown in the Portable Hellforge draft notes — worth double-checking against the actual sprue before finalizing base-topper sizes.)

Command models scale 1-per-10: each Cohort unit carries its own Musician (Gong Carrier), Standard Bearer, and Champion — worth checking the sprue/instructions for whether the box actually includes parts for a Standard Bearer and Champion pose, since the box copy only explicitly calls out the Gong Carrier.

**Daemonsmith / Ashen Elder** is a single dual-build kit — one model per box, alternate build, not two-in-one. Plan: buy one box, build it as whichever one is needed first (see the 1,250-point bracket in the [AoS Army List](army-aos/)), then convert the spare 2nd War Despot into the other as a kitbash/counts-as instead of buying a second box.

## Spearhead-legal subset

For actual Spearhead games, only one specific loadout is legal: War Despot, Dominator Engine with Immolation Cannons, Tormentor Bombard (standard), Infernal Cohort with Hashutite Spears + Gong-bearer (one 10-model unit only — the 2nd Cohort, 2nd Dominator, and Deathshrieker aren't part of the fixed Spearhead composition). See [AoS Army List](army-aos/) for full matched-play games where everything above comes into play.
