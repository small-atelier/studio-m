## Paint Scheme

Concept: black-iron industrial forge with worn bronze fittings and a daemonfire glow — restrained dark palette, small hot accents, per the original brainstorm.

### Table-top pass

1. Undercoat — **Vallejo Surface Primer, NATO Green**
2. Highlight drybrush — **Army Painter Matt White**, heavy drybrush from a high angle (zenithal-style)
   *(drybrush is faster and more forgiving, airbrush zenithal keeps panel lines crisper on the Dominator Engine's flat plates; can mix both across the roster)*
3. Drybrush metal trim — **Army Painter Weapon Bronze**
4. Recess/rune highlights — **Liquitex Titanium White Ink**, fine brush into runes, vents, sigils
5. Green glaze over the white highlights to tint them and make them pop — thin **Army Painter Angel Green**
   *(guess: Angel Green is the brightest/cleanest green on hand; Jungle Green or Goblin Green would read darker/muddier — swap if a different pop is wanted)*
6. Table-top ready — stop here for first games.

### Later detail pass

- Pick out rivets/chains/eyes in **Greedy Gold** or **Shining Silver**
- Wash recesses with **Strong Tone** or **Dark Tone** Quickshade
- Apply the Hashut icon and ziggurat transfers from the included transfer sheet
- Optional: translucent resin + LED glow on furnace/molten-crack details, matching the Portable Hellforge build (`content/posts/portable-hellforge`, still draft)

## Materials Used

- Vallejo Surface Primer — NATO Green
- Army Painter Matt White
- Army Painter Weapon Bronze
- Liquitex Titanium White Ink
- Army Painter Angel Green
