## Concept

A small carved-stone flame finial on a plinth base — an attachment/scatter piece for topping walls, balustrades, and pyramid tier corners, not a standalone terrain feature on its own.

![Prospero Brazier concept](brazier.png)

## Features

- Short square plinth base with deep-carved Thousand Sons arcanotech glyph banding
- Tall stylized stone flame finial rising from the plinth top — carved solid stone, not lit/glowing
- Weathered, textured stone finish matching the rest of the Prospero board
- Sized to attach along wall tops, balustrade corners, or pyramid tier edges

## Best materials

- **3D printer / resin** — whole piece, single-piece cast (plinth + flame finial together)

## Build idea

Print a batch of these as a repeatable detail piece — line them along pyramid tier corners, obelisk plinths, or ruin wall tops for a consistent motif across the board. Paint and weather to match whichever piece they're attached to; no assembly beyond gluing to the host piece's edge.

## Generator scripts

- [`brazier.py`](brazier.py) — base plinth + carved flame finial as a single piece
- [`brazier_with_flame.py`](brazier_with_flame.py) — combined variant with the flame geometry merged in
- [`flame_only.py`](flame_only.py) — flame finial alone, for swapping onto other plinths
