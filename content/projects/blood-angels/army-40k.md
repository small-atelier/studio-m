## Notes / Ideas

- Theme: Death Company / Sanguinary Guard aggressive melee — jump-pack units up the board fast, Assault Intercessors hold the objectives behind them.
- Detachment guess: **Angels of Death** or a Sanguinius-successor detachment leaning into Death Company synergy — needs checking against the current Faction Pack once this actually gets built.
- Warlord progression: Chaplain through 500p–2000p, replaced by **Mephiston, Lord of Death** at 3000p.
- Sanguinary Priest is the glue — attach to whichever melee block needs the Feel No Pain most that turn.
- Land Raider Redeemer + Furioso Dreadnought is the mid-game punch-through package once the collection reaches 2000p.
