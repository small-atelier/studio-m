## Build Notes

- Paint the disc following the [Screamer scheme](screamers-paint) — same NATO Green / Crystal Blue / Alien Purple workflow, yellow glow on the front
- Recess a 2mm magnet into the top of each disc for the Herald of Tzeentch
- Mount disc on 60mm base using the same [dark stone scheme](base/) as the rest of the force


### Disc of Tzeentch

Follow the [Screamer paint scheme](screamers-paint) — NATO Green primer, Crystal Blue from below, Alien Purple from above, Daemonic Yellow glow on the front, ink glazes to finish.
