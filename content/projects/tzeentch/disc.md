## Paint Scheme (the plan at least so far)

Darker and more architectural than the screamers. The white drybrush over NATO Green creates a pre-built light/dark structure — the transparent inks then tint it, with the raised parts reading brighter and the recesses staying dark green. Trim is where it gets its character: black in the grooves, gold on the edges.

### 1. Prime

**Vallejo Surface Primer – NATO Green**

Dark undertone — shadows already built in.

### 2. Drybrush — White

**Matt White**, drybrushed over the whole model.

Hits the raised surfaces and edges. The contrast between bright white peaks and dark green recesses is what the inks will work with.

### 3. Ink Filter — Blue from Below

**Liquitex Phthalocyanine Blue Ink**, sprayed from below.

The white picks it up brightly on the underside; the green recesses barely shift. Cool warp blue on the lower surfaces.

### 4. Ink Filter — Purple from Above

**Purple ink**, sprayed from above.

Tints the top surfaces and raised detail. Combined with the blue below, the body reads dark and shifting — green in the depths, blue and purple in the light.

### 5. Trim — Black Base

Paint all raised trim, runes, and rim with **Black**.

Work carefully — this is what separates the body from the gold. The black acts as both a recess liner and a base coat for the gold.

### 6. Trim — Gold

**Gold metallic** layered over the black trim.

Leave the black showing in the deepest cuts — the contrast between dark groove and bright gold edge is the whole effect.
