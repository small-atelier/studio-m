This will give a **bright warp look for Screamers of Tzeentch serving Tzeentch**.

---

# 3-Pass Screamer Scheme

*(Primer + 3 airbrush passes)*

## Prime (color primer instead of white/black)

Use:

**Vallejo Surface Primer – NATO Green**

Why this works:

* green gives a **weird chaotic undertone**
* purple and blue over it look magical
* shadows are already done

Don’t fully cover every corner — slight unevenness looks organic.

---

# Airbrush Pass 1 — Warp Belly

Spray from **below the model**.

Paint:

* **Army Painter Crystal Blue**

Focus on:

* belly
* lower fins
* tail underside

Let some green remain in shadows.

Result:
green shadows → turquoise belly.

---

# Airbrush Pass 2 — Top Skin

Spray from **above**.

Paint:

* **Army Painter Alien Purple**

Focus on:

* back
* top fins
* tail

Leave blue showing on the sides.

Now you have:

**green → blue → purple gradient**

---

# Airbrush Pass 3 — Warp Glow

Light spray from **front/top**.

Paint:

* **Army Painter Daemonic Yellow**

Hit only:

* nose
* mouth area
* front fins

This creates **instant magical color contrast**.

---

# Optional 30-second magic (worth it)

Glaze lightly with inks:

* **Liquitex Phthalocyanine Blue Ink** → underside
* **Liquitex Quinacridone Magenta Ink** → back

It blends everything instantly.

---

# 2-minute details

Teeth:
**Skeleton Bone**

Eyes:
**Daemonic Yellow**

Mouth:
**Warlock Purple**

---

# Result

You should get:

* green shadow undertone
* turquoise belly
* purple back
* yellow magical face glow

Very **Tzeentch energy**.
