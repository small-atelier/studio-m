## Paint Scheme

Follow the [Pink Horror scheme](pink-horrors) — black primer, white drybrush, Magenta ink body. Add extra detail time on the gold staff and eyes to read as a hero rather than a trooper. 

### Body

Same as Pink Horrors:
1. **Black primer**
2. Heavy drybrush **Matt White**
3. **Liquitex Quinacridone Magenta Ink** (airbrush, selective)
4. Lighter drybrush **Matt White**
5. Light pass of **Liquitex Phthalocyanine Blue Ink** on highlights

### Hero Details

More time on the details than a rank-and-file Horror:

- **Staff and ornaments** — metallic gold, wash with dark ink, highlight with gold again
- **Cloak or scroll work** (if present) — **Skeleton Bone**, shade with **Burnt Umber**
- **Eyes** — **Daemonic Yellow** or bright orange glaze for a glowing herald look
- **Beak and claws** — **Skeleton Bone** with **Burnt Umber** wash

The extra time on gold and eyes is what separates a herald from a rank trooper.
