## Paint Scheme

Departs from the Pink Horror body recipe used elsewhere — grey primer instead of black, and an umbra shadow ink pass before the colour goes on.


### Body

1. **Grey primer**
2. **Umbra ink** — shadow pass
3. **Magenta ink**
4. **White ink** for highlights — used as a filter/reactivate pass, brings some pink back up out of the magenta

### Staff & Metals

1. **Black** — base/shadow coat for the metal areas
2. **Bright gold** over the black

Staff shaft itself:
1. **White** base
2. **Skeleton Bone** over the top
3. Brown wash to follow (not yet done)

### Horns

1. Same grey primer → umbra ink → magenta ink → white ink (filter/reactivate) pass as the body
2. **Blue ink + White ink**, mixed, painted over
3. **Skeleton Bone** highlights
