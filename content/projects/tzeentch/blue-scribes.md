## Notes

A unique model with two sub-schemes: the [Blue Horror](blue-horrors) riders (blue ink over white base) and the Disc (follow the [Screamer scheme](screamers-paint)). The scrolls and quills are the character detail — **Skeleton Bone** with **Burnt Umber** wash, ink writing picked out in a dark colour.
