An upgraded Flamer of Tzeentch — larger, more chaotic, and on foot rather than grouped in a unit. Using an older retro-style sculpt (no attached flame effect) — 3 copies being printed.

Part of the [modular magnet system](magnetization) — the same Exalted Flamer body on a plain base is a standalone Exalted Flamer, on a chariot base it becomes a Burning Chariot.
