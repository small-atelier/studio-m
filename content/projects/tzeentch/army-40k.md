## Notes / Ideas

- Core of the collection is already Tzeentch-flavoured shooting/psychic: [Pink Horrors](pink-horrors) → [Blue Horrors](blue-horrors) → [Brimstone Horrors](brimstone-paint) split chain gives durable objective-holding troops for relatively few points.
- [Screamers](screamers-paint) and [Flamers](flamers) are the fast/deep-strike elements — Screamers threaten backfield objectives, Flamers punish grouped infantry.
- [Lord of Change](lord-of-change) or [Kairos Fateweaver](kairos-fateweaver) as the single big psychic threat/beatstick — probably not both in the same list at this collection's size.
- Heralds ([Changecaster](herald-of-tzeentch) on foot, [Fluxmaster](herald-of-tzeentch) on [disc](disc), [Fateskimmer](herald-of-tzeentch) on [chariot](chariot)) are the cheap character glue — attach to whichever unit needs the buff/screen.
- [The Changeling](the-changeling) and [The Blue Scribes](blue-scribes) are the utility/support pieces — worth checking current detachment rules before relying on them, since the Blue Scribes has no current 11th-edition datasheet (see Roster notes).
