## Approximate Paint Scheme (reverse-engineered)

Recipe is a guess based on the finished model — not confirmed.

### 1. Prime

**Grey primer**

Not black — grey keeps the blues vivid and prevents the model from reading too dark overall.

### 2. Body — Airbrush

**Army Painter Crystal Blue** over the whole model.

### 3. Body — Ink

**Liquitex Phthalocyanine Blue Ink** into the recesses.

Pushes the scale shadows deep and adds iridescence on the raised surfaces.

### 4. Body — Highlight

Light drybrush of **Crystal Blue + Matt White** on the raised scale edges.

### 5. Wings — Split Airbrush

**Army Painter Crystal Blue** from the outer tips inward.

**Army Painter Alien Purple** from the root/body junction outward.

The two meet in the middle — same split-zone technique as the flamers. Blue dominates, purple at the root gives the warm violet shift.

### 6. Gold Details

**Metallic gold** on the staff ornaments and armour trim.

Wash with a dark ink to shade.

### 7. Eyes

**Daemonic Yellow**
