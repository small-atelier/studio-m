## Approximate Paint Scheme (reverse-engineered)

Recipe is a guess based on the finished models — not confirmed.

### 1. Prime

**Black primer**

### 2. Drybrush — Round 1

**Matt White** — medium-heavy pass over all raised surfaces.

### 3. Ink — Split coverage (airbrush)

**Liquitex Quinacridone Magenta Ink** on one zone (e.g. lower body, mouth, flame tongues).

**Liquitex Phthalocyanine Blue Ink** on the opposite zone (e.g. upper body, back, horns).

Apply so the two inks meet and bleed into each other in the middle — the overlap becomes a natural blue-violet blend.

### 4. Drybrush — Round 2

**Matt White**, lighter pass.

Re-brightens highlights on top of the ink layers. The white picks up both colors slightly and softens the blend further.

### 5. Ink — Round 2 (airbrush)

Repeat the split — reinforce **Magenta** on the warm zones, **Blue** on the cool zones.

Builds saturation and pushes the contrast between the two halves.

### 6. Drybrush — Round 3

**Matt White**, very light final pass on the highest points only.

### 7. Details — White

**Matt White** for claws, teeth, flame tips. Residual ink on the brush will tint them slightly — that's fine.

### 8. Eyes

first **Matt White**, then a small dot of **red**.
