## Notes / Ideas

- Theme: vanilla Gladius-style combined arms — no chapter-specific gimmick, just a solid Intercessor/Hellblaster/armour core.
- Detachment guess: **Gladius Task Force** — generic, flexible, good default for a first-ever list; revisit once the collection has a clearer identity.
- Warlord progression: Captain through 500p–2000p, replaced by **Roboute Guilliman** at 3000p.
- Librarian is the glue — smite/buff whichever unit is under the most pressure that turn.
- Gladiator Lancer + Repulsor is the mid-game armour package once the collection reaches 2000p.
