{{< lead >}}
A home-built, double-sided 22" × 30" Spearhead board, the size the Spearhead rules give. One face copies Aqshy and the other Ghryan, the two sides of the Fire and Jade realm battlefield.
{{< /lead >}}

{{< figure src="board_layers.png" alt="Exploded concept sketch of the board layers" caption="Concept sketch of the layer stack — double-sided hardboard core, carved foam, flush tile mosaic with objective discs." >}}

## The board

- Hardboard base, framed edge.
- Double-sided: Aqshy on one face, Ghryan on the other.
- Foam glued on top: terrain detail (cracks, etc.) cut downward into the foam, separate foam tiles + objectives set on top, kept flush with the surrounding surface. Sealed with acrylic floor primer, see [on XPS foam]({{< ref "/notes/floor-primer-as-hobby-medium#on-xps-foam" >}}).
- Corners capped with printed [corner protectors]({{< ref "/printables/board-corner-protectors" >}}).

## Objectives

The objectives follow the official ring layouts: Aqshy has five 6" rings, Ghryan three 6" rings plus two that run off the side edges. Layouts and board photos are in the [battlefield reference]({{< ref "/notes/spearhead-battlefield-reference#official-realm-battlefields" >}}).

Each ring is a 6" [objective marker tile]({{< ref "/printables/objective-marker-tiles" >}}) set into the foam mosaic at the same top height, with Ghryan's two side objectives as edge pieces. A resin-printed 40mm coin is glued flush into each tile's pocket: five designs from the **Spearhead Objective Markers (FDM-friendly)** set, one per godbeast (dragon face, dragon side, phoenix, snake, skull). Which coin goes where is in the [battlefield reference]({{< ref "/notes/spearhead-battlefield-reference#official-realm-battlefields" >}}).

## Terrain

2 large + 2 small features, per the Spearhead rules. The pieces and their print status are tracked in [AoS Terrain]({{< ref "/projects/aos-terrain" >}}).
