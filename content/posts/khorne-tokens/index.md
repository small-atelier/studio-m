## What's in the kit

The Gore Pilgrims Spearhead is a Slaughterpriest, 2× 5 Blood Warriors, 10 Bloodreavers and 3 Mighty Skullcrushers. Most of the army's turn-to-turn state is small effects that are easy to forget, so the tokens act as physical **reminders** of which ability did what, not just as state markers.

**Hex tokens** (33mm, pointy-top): the front shows the effect (Khorne rune, a big value and a short label). The back shows the ability that caused it, mirrored so it reads correctly when flipped.

| Token | Front | Back | Print |
|---|---|---|---|
| Heads Must Roll | +1 REND | HEADS MUST ROLL | ×3 |
| Unholy Flames | +1 REND | UNHOLY FLAMES | ×1 |
| Murderlust | D6" MOVE | MURDER LUST | ×3 |
| Generic | rune | rune | spares |

*Heads Must Roll* (3 Blood Tithe) gives up to 3 units +1 Rend until the start of your next turn, so that's three tokens. *Unholy Flames* is the enhancement version of the same effect on a single unit. *Murderlust* moves D3 units D6" immediately. It doesn't leave lasting state, but it's easy to forget to use, so it gets a token too.

**Damage trays** (×6): an arrow-shaped plate, 22×40×6.5mm, with a pocket for one 16mm d6. The die shows the damage on the unit's currently wounded model, and the arrow points at the unit. It's single-sided, with the Khorne rune on the arrowhead.

## The box

The box has the same footprint as the Hashut box (165.5×131.2×24.5mm) and uses the same column layout. Every well is 22mm deep:

- **Left column:** 3 stacked hex wells, one per token type. Each well has a lift notch through the left wall so a token can be pinched out from the side.
- **Right column, front:** a 123×73mm slot for the warscroll cards, with a notch through the front wall. The floor carries a cards + die icon because the slot works just as well as dice storage.
- **Right column, back:** 2 arrow-shaped wells holding 3 damage trays each (notched through the back wall), plus the Blood Tithe counter.

**Blood Tithe wheels:** two 30mm digit wheels (tens and units, 0–99), each spinning on a 6mm peg that's part of the floor. The digits read from the top, with a red pointer in front of each wheel and a **BLOOD TITHE** label under both. The wheels sit 0.5mm inside the back wall, and a 20mm slot cut through the wall at each wheel lets you thumb it round from outside the box. Each wheel sits 1mm below the rim, so the closed lid holds it in place.

**Lid:** it slips over the base with a full-height skirt, with the Khorne rune on top. The inside face carries a dice icon: flip the lid over and it's a dice tray.

## Downloads

Each piece comes as a base + inlay STL pair, an `_anycubic.3mf` (Anycubic Slicer Next's own per-part filament metadata) and a plain `.3mf`. Everything prints in two colors (default: black in slot 1, red in slot 2). Set `BASE_EXTRUDER_SLOT`/`INLAY_EXTRUDER_SLOT` in the scripts to whatever's loaded. Print the tithe wheel test coupon first to check the wheel's fit on your printer.
