The all-Tzaangor Disciples of Tzeentch Spearhead: a Shaman on Disc with Enlightened, Skyfires and a Tzaangor herd.
{{< /lead >}}

## Force

- **General:** Tzaangor Shaman on Disc
- 3 Tzaangor Enlightened
- 3 Tzaangor Skyfires
- 10 Tzaangors: from the [Thousand Sons]({{< ref "/armies/thousand-sons/roster" >}})

Tzaangors are Disciples of Tzeentch in Age of Sigmar and Thousand Sons in 40K, so the force draws on both the [Tzeentch]({{< ref "/armies/tzeentch" >}}) and [Thousand Sons]({{< ref "/armies/thousand-sons" >}}) armies.
