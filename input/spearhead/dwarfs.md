🖨️ Can you 3D-print the bases?

Yes. You can print decorative bases or base toppers and put the models on them.

The important distinction is:

The model's official base size matters for the game.

You don't want to make a 32mm model into effectively a 50mm footprint because it looks cool. That can affect movement, objective control, melee range, coherency, etc.

But you can absolutely have:

official-size base → 3D-printed decorative surface → model

So visually you get almost complete freedom while keeping the correct gameplay footprint.

You can even design your own bases in Blender/Tinkercad/Fusion/etc. and print them yourself.

And there's a really cool possibility...

You could make every base tell a tiny story.

Then the whole Spearhead looks like a single diorama when it's deployed.

One thing I'd avoid

Don't make it too tall.

You want the box to remain something you can actually carry comfortably.

I'd aim for roughly:

35–45 cm wide

25–30 cm deep

12–18 cm tall

depending on the exact models and how much terrain you want.

The lid could then contain the tallest/most fragile pieces or removable scenery.

But I'd measure the actual assembled Spearhead models first before committing to dimensions.
