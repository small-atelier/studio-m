Summary:
This item is part of my Dwarven AleWorks collectionThis is a boil kettle and tun system for boiling wort and passing it into a tun to settle. It's mounted on some hewn stone, with a small furnace built-in below, and a spigot on the tun.Prints with no supports, pic is printed @ .12mm layer height. The spigots print separately as a small item, then glue on with a guide tab. If you have trouble with the tab fitting due to tight tolerances, just trim it off with a utility knife and glue on (you just have to align it yourself).About the Dwarven AleWorksThis set was made possible by my amazing Kickstarter backers from the recent Forest of Oakenspire Kickstarter!
